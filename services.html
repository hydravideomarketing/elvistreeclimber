Tree service FAQs

How much does tree removal or trimming cost in Palm Coast, FL?

The price depends on the size and condition of the tree, how hard it is to reach, and how much cleanup is needed. Elvis offers free estimates, so call 386-569-0163 or send a quote request and you will get a straight answer before any work starts.

What is the difference between tree trimming and tree pruning?

Trimming shapes and tidies your trees for looks and clearance, while pruning removes dead, diseased, or hazardous branches to protect the tree’s health and structure.

Do you handle storm damage and fallen trees?

Yes. Elvis handles storm cleanup for fallen limbs and hazardous trees across Palm Coast and Flagler County, a common need during Florida’s hurricane season.

Can you remove a tree that is close to my house or power lines?

Yes. Climbing work with rope and saddle lets Elvis reach trees in tight spaces that larger equipment can’t, and a bucket lift is used when it’s the safer choice. Call for a free on-site estimate.

Do you grind stumps after removing a tree?

Yes. Stump grinding finishes the job so you can reclaim your yard. You can request it together with a removal or on its own.

Do you work on commercial properties and HOA communities?

Yes. Elvis provides tree trimming, removal, storm cleanup and stump grinding for businesses, HOAs and rental properties in Palm Coast and the surrounding Flagler County area.

What areas do you serve?

Palm Coast, FL and the surrounding areas, including Flagler County. Call 386-569-0163 to confirm service to your address.
